Dear Prof.\ Karthik Raman and Editorial Board,

I am pleased to submit the second revision of the above manuscript in
response to the reviewers' reports.
Reviewer~1 indicated that all previous concerns have been fully addressed.
For Reviewer~2, I have corrected all identified technical errors and
provided precise rebuttals where the concerns reflect a mischaracterisation
of the stochastic Petri net formalism or of contextual Petri net theory.
The most substantive correction addresses signal arc cycles in the
\textit{B.~subtilis} model: four enzyme-regeneration arcs were
reclassified from signal-flow to normal type, eliminating all $G_s$
cycles.
The corrected model has been deposited on Zenodo
(DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21478129, version v2.7.1), and re-validation
confirms numerically identical results.
A detailed point-by-point response is provided in the accompanying
\textit{Response to Reviewers} document.

I believe the revised manuscript is now technically sound and ready for
editorial evaluation.
