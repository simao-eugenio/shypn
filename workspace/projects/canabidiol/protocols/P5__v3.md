> **Pairing.** Model: TBD — likely `cbd_ad_neuroprotection_v3_p5.shy`

## Hypothesis

Cytokine and NF-κB transients in `[0, 1800] s` carry the actual
pharmacological signal that endpoint-only data drops, and the
transient shape depends on whether the cell is healthy, MCI, or fully
diseased.

## Viability panel — sweep plan

**Mode:** Factorial • **Replicates:** 30 • **Duration:** 1800 s (30 min) • **Termination:** time

> Time resolution: high. Use small `max_tau` (e.g. 0.01) to capture
> sub-second transients. Output statistics file will be larger than
> the 4 h protocols on a per-cell basis (more snapshots per second).

| Sweep parameter | Path | Values | Levels |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `[param] Disease_Severity` | `Disease_Severity.initial_marking` | 0, 2 | 2 |
| `[param] LOADING_DOSE` | `LOADING_DOSE.initial_marking` | 0, 3, 10 | 3 |
| `[param] Age` | `Age.initial_marking` | 65, 85 | 2 |

**Cells:** 2 × 3 × 2 = **12** • **Total sims:** 12 × 30 = **360**
