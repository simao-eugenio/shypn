## Hypothesis

A loading bolus + repeated maintenance top-ups every `DOSE_INTERVAL`
seconds rescues neuron health in a disease-severity-dependent and
age-dependent manner. P1 explicitly varies `Disease_Severity` so both
*whether* a patient needs treatment and *whether* it works are mapped
on the same grid.

## Results — `run_20260424_005438`

5760/5760 OK, ~20 min wall per cell. Findings F1-F8 documented in
[`docs/p1_deep_analysis_run_20260424_005438.md`](../docs/p1_deep_analysis_run_20260424_005438.md):

- **F1.** Aβ-plaque lock-in detected in 93/1152 cells at LD ≥ 5 → motivates P4 bifurcation map.
- **F2.** NH variance partition: LD ≈ 50 %, Age ≈ 28 %, Sev ≈ 3 %. Sev contribution is suspiciously small → motivates P6 calibration.
- **F4.** Age dominates Severity ≈ 2:1; Age 65 → 85 costs ~18 NeuH points, Sev 0 → 3 costs ~10.
- **F6.** Drug saturates by LD ≈ 5–10, MT ≈ 1–3. Anything above LD = 10 / MT = 3 wastes budget.
- **F7.** Age-85 ceiling: even at saturating dose, NH plateaus ~70 → population that can never reach LD-65 outcome.
- **F8.** Plaque is reversible under maintenance dosing (peaks at t ≈ 2 h, falling to ≈ 9.5 by t = 4 h with `(LD=0, MT=3)`).

**Implications for P2/P3:** trim dose grid to `LD ∈ {0, 2, 5, 10}`, `MT ∈ {0, 0.3, 1, 3}`.
