## Hypothesis (and audit)

Validate the `evt_install_disease` calibration by sweeping
`Disease_Severity ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3}` with no drug, and
inspecting steady-state markers. Severity=0 must remain healthy;
Severity=2 must reproduce v2 AD baseline within tight tolerance.

Directly addresses P1 finding **F2** (Severity contributes only ~3 %
of NH variance) — possible cause: the cascade self-ignites from M₀
even at Sev=0, leaving little severity-dependent signal at t = 4 h.
