# Protocol P2 — Withdrawal challenge across severities

> **Pairing.** Model: [`models/cbd_ad_neuroprotection_v3.shy`](../models/cbd_ad_neuroprotection_v3.shy)
> (38 places, 45 transitions, 18 events). Sweep injects an additional
> `evt_washout` event to zero `CBD_extracellular` at `t = 5400 s`.
> The model's built-in `evt_maint_{1,2,3}` will still fire after
> washout — this is the **intended withdrawal challenge** (drug
> off but cellular memory of dosing remains).

## Hypothesis

Without redose, the inflammatory flare and plaque re-accumulation rate
after washout characterise the *withdrawal cost*, and that cost scales
with disease severity.
