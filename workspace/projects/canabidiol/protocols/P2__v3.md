# Protocol P2 — Withdrawal challenge across severities

> **Pairing.** Model: [`models/cbd_ad_neuroprotection_v3_p2.shy`](../models/cbd_ad_neuroprotection_v3_p2.shy)
> (v3 variant: `MAINT_DOSE=0`, `DOSE_INTERVAL=1e9`, `evt_washout`
> baked in at `t > 5400 s`). The model's `evt_maint_*` events are
> retained but never fire because `DOSE_INTERVAL` is pinned huge —
> intentional: the place-marking history of dosing remains, only the
> drug input is removed at washout time.

## Hypothesis

Without redose, the inflammatory flare and plaque re-accumulation rate
after washout characterise the *withdrawal cost*, and that cost scales
with disease severity.
