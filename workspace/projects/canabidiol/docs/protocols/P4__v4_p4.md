## Hypothesis

A bistable boundary exists between **clearing** and **locked** plaque
trajectories, located at a severity- and age-dependent critical
loading dose. Map it with refined LD resolution and enough replicates
per cell to classify each (Sev, LD, Age) cell as
"reliably clears" / "mixed" / "reliably locks".

## Origin

Refines finding **F1** in
[`docs/p1_deep_analysis_run_20260424_005438.md`](../p1_deep_analysis_run_20260424_005438.md)
— Aβ-plaque lock-in detected in 93/1152 cells of P1 at LD ≥ 5.

## Model details

Derived from `cbd_ad_neuroprotection_v3.shy` by removing
`evt_maint_{1,2,3}`, giving a pure single-bolus dispatch. The
`MAINT_DOSE` and `DOSE_INTERVAL` parameter places still exist but
are inert without their consuming events, so they are pinned by the
sweep (DOSE_INTERVAL = 4300 s) and not varied.

## Viability Panel — sweep plan

**Mode:** Factorial  •  **Replicates:** 60  •  **Duration:** 14400 s = **4 h**  •  **Termination:** time

> The 4 h window matches P1 so the same lock-in detector and
> end-of-run classifier apply directly. The descending limb of plaque
> clearance under maintenance dosing (finding F2b, requires ≥ 8 h) is
> *not* in scope here — covered by sub-protocol P1L.

| Sweep parameter | Path | Values | Levels |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `[param] Disease_Severity` | `Disease_Severity.initial_marking` | 1, 2, 3       | 3 |
| `[param] LOADING_DOSE`     | `LOADING_DOSE.initial_marking`     | 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 | 5 |
| `[param] DOSE_INTERVAL`    | `DOSE_INTERVAL.initial_marking`    | 4300          | 1 |
| `[param] Age`              | `Age.initial_marking`              | 65, 75, 85    | 3 |

**Cells:** 3 × 5 × 1 × 3 = **45**  •  **Total sims:** 45 × 60 = **2700**
