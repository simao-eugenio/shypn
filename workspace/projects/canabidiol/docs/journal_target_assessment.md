## Publisher Hub Assessment

### 1. Royal Society Publishing — BEST FIT

| Journal | IF | Systems Biology | Bioinformatics | Drug Discovery | Petri Nets | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **J. Royal Society Interface** | 3.5 | **Explicit subject** | **Computational biology** subject | Indirect | **Biomathematics** subject | **Excellent** |
| **Interface Focus** | 4.0 | **Explicit subject** | **Computational biology** subject | Indirect | Themed issues on biological network dynamics | **Excellent** |
| Royal Society Open Science | 2.9 | Accepted | Accepted | Accepted | Accepted | Good (broad) |
| Open Biology | — | Partial | No | No | No | Weak |

**Key findings:**
- **J. Royal Society Interface** lists **systems biology**, **computational biology**, and **biomathematics** as explicit subject categories. The CBD model paper (hybrid HPN + dose-response + phase transitions) is a textbook fit.
- **Interface Focus** publishes themed issues — they recently had one on *"Combinatorial models for evolving representation of dynamical behaviours in biological networks"* — practically written for Petri net modeling of signaling pathways.
- **APC: FREE in 2026** — Royal Society announced all journals are open access through Subscribe to Open, meaning no APC for authors. This is the strongest financial argument.

**Verdict: Primary target. Two strong journal options, zero APC cost.**

### 2. IEEE — GOOD FIT (for the tool/formalism paper)

| Journal | Systems Biology | Bioinformatics | Drug Discovery | Petri Nets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IEEE/ACM Trans. Comp. Bio. & Bioinformatics** (TCBB) | Good | **Core scope** | Indirect | Computational methods |
| IEEE Trans. Systems, Man, Cybernetics | Partial | Partial | No | **Formal methods** |
| IEEE Access | Broad | Broad | Broad | Broad |

**Key findings:**
- **TCBB** is the best fit for a SHYPN tool paper (framework, hybrid simulation engine, tau-leaping + ODE integration). It publishes computational biology methods and tools.
- IEEE is strong for the **Petri net formalism** contribution (stochastic hybrid HPN theory, adaptive transitions) — this community publishes extensively in IEEE venues.
- **Weak for the biological/pharmacological results** (CBD model paper). IEEE reviewers would want more CS/engineering content.

**Verdict: Good secondary target, specifically for a SHYPN methods/tool paper or a Petri net theory paper. Not ideal for the CBD biological results.**

---

### 3. ACS (American Chemical Society) — MODERATE FIT

| Journal | Systems Biology | Bioinformatics | Drug Discovery | Petri Nets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACS Chemical Neuroscience | Partial | No | **Neuropharmacology** | No |
| J. Chemical Information & Modeling (JCIM) | Partial | **Computational modeling** | **Drug modeling** | No |
| ACS Pharmacology & Transl. Science | No | No | **Pharmacology** | No |

**Key findings:**
- ACS is **chemistry-centric**. The work has a pharmacological angle (CBD dose-response, therapeutic windows, EC50 estimation) but the core methodology is systems biology/Petri nets, not molecular chemistry.
- **JCIM** could work if the paper is framed as "computational pharmacology modeling" — but they typically expect molecular-level (docking, QSAR, MD) rather than pathway-level models.
- **ACS Chemical Neuroscience** could consider the CBD/neuroinflammation angle, but they lean toward experimental validation with wet-lab data.

**Verdict: Partial fit at best. Would require significant reframing away from core strengths (Petri nets, systems biology) toward chemistry/pharmacology. Not recommended as primary target.**

### 4. IOP Publishing / Microbiology Society — WEAK FIT

| Journal | Systems Biology | Bioinformatics | Drug Discovery | Petri Nets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physical Biology (IOP) | Physics approaches to biology | Partial | No | No |
| Bioinspiration & Biomimetics | No | No | No | No |
| Microbiology Society journals | No | No | No | No |

**Key findings:**
- **Physical Biology** could theoretically accept a quantitative biology paper, but its community expects physics-first approaches (statistical mechanics, reaction-diffusion PDEs) rather than Petri net formalism.
- **Microbiology Society** journals (Microbiology, J. General Virology, etc.) are entirely wrong domain — focused on microorganisms, not neurodegeneration/neuroprotection.

**Verdict: Not recommended. Neither IOP nor Microbiology Society journals align with our domains.**

1. **CBD model paper** → *J. Royal Society Interface* (systems biology + computational biology + biomathematics, IF 3.5, zero APC)
