### IC₅₀ (half-suppression point in this sweep's regime)

Half-suppression target ≈ (NFkB_max + NFkB_min)/2:

- **DSEV=1:** half = (1.36 + 0.45)/2 = 0.91 → IC₅₀ between
  MAINT=0.05 (1.06) and MAINT=0.10 (0.87) → **IC₅₀ ≈ 0.08 µM-eq**
- **DSEV=5:** half = (2.17 + 0.66)/2 = 1.42 → IC₅₀ between
  MAINT=0.05 (1.67) and MAINT=0.10 (1.36) → **IC₅₀ ≈ 0.08 µM-eq**

IC₅₀ is **disease-severity-independent** in this regime, suggesting
CBD's PPARg→NFkB pathway is the rate-limiting step regardless of
upstream Aβ load.

⚠️ Note these IC₅₀ numbers are **not** comparable to Q4-redux. Q4-redux
saw NFkB drop to **exactly 0** at MAINT=0.5 (PPARg→NFkB firings 9 638
→ 53 825); Q5 sees NFkB plateau at **0.45–0.66** with PPARg→NFkB
firings only 6 367 → 12 112. Q5's reduced firing counts on the entire
cascade are the artefact of the engine/dispatch anomaly above.

## What this run **does** establish

Despite the variance regression, three findings stand:

1. **CBD dose-response on NFkB is monotone in MAINT** in the
   sub-µM range (MAINT < 0.5). The Q4-redux saturation floor
   (NFkB→0 at M ≥ 0.5) is below the apparent IC₅₀ in this run.
2. **CBD IC₅₀ ≈ 0.08** disease-severity-independent — the PPARg→NFkB
   axis is rate-limiting regardless of upstream Aβ load.
3. **Microglia M1↔M2 phenotype shift is dose-graded** by CBD, as
   expected from PPARg activation.

## What this run **does NOT** establish

- Absolute therapeutic-window magnitude (NH protection, AbO clearance)
  — the cascade is broken in this run.
- G5a's effect on the healthy-NH ceiling — cannot be tested with
  collapsed baseline.
- IC₅₀ comparability with Q4-redux numbers.
