FUJITA-1  —  Direct Fujita replication: medium × route factorial        (Q1)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Question: Does the medium (ATP level) determine commitment, not just the
            route of Spo0A activation?
  Prediction: SM-like (N0=100) + abrupt pulse → high sporulation;
              CH-like (N0=1440) + abrupt pulse → low sporulation (~5%)

  Replicates per condition : 100
  Total conditions         : 5

  DIMENSION 1 — INITIAL_NUTRIENTS (P27)  [proxy for medium]
    100   µM  →  SM-like  (nutrient-depleted, ATP will fall)
    1440  µM  →  CH-like  (nutrient-rich, ATP stays high)

  DIMENSION 2 — LOADING_DOSE (P37)  [route of Spo0A activation]
    0     µM  →  Natural phosphorelay (no pulse)
    27    µM  →  Abrupt Spo0A* pulse at t=1s (INTERVENTION_TIME_S=1)

  CONDITIONS (name | N0 | LOADING_DOSE | INTERVENTION_TIME_S):
    Baseline              |  100 |  0  | 99999   ← SM natural (100% sporulate)
    SM_natural            |  100 |  0  | 99999   (same as Baseline for reference)
    SM_abrupt             |  100 | 27  |     1   ← KEY PREDICTION: should be >>5%
    CH_natural            | 1440 |  0  | 99999   ← ~48% sporulate
    CH_abrupt             | 1440 | 27  |     1   ← Fujita Fig.2C/D equivalent (~5%)

  Expected results:
    SM_natural  : ~100%  (positive control)
    SM_abrupt   : >30%   (PREDICTION — abrupt works if medium is depleted)
    CH_natural  : ~48%   (bet-hedging, confirmed from Sweep C)
    CH_abrupt   : ~5%    (Fujita experimental result, should be reproduced)
