  KEY FINDINGS:

  1. SM_abrupt = 100% confirms Q1 medium prediction:
     abrupt Spo0A_P pulse in nutrient-depleted medium (N0=100) → 100% sporulation.
     ATP depletes naturally → cell crosses the vegetative basin floor (2.13 mM)
     regardless of how Spo0A_P was triggered.

  2. CH_abrupt = 56% ≠ Fujita ~5% — IMPORTANT CAVEAT:
     LOADING_DOSE=27 is a SINGLE Spo0A_P spike at t=1s, subject to nutrient-
     boosted phosphatase (rate = 0.01 × Spo0A_P × (1 + 9×N/(100+N))).
     This is NOT equivalent to Fujita's constitutively active Spo0A* (phospho-
     mimetic, bypasses phosphatase entirely). To replicate the ~5% result
     we would need a sustained Spo0A_P source in rich medium. The pulse is
     cleared at CH nutrient levels (phosphatase ~9.4× vs SM) but not fast
     enough — some Spo0A_P persists and helps drive sporulation to 56%.
     Conclusion: our model captures the direction (SM>>CH) but not the exact
     magnitude of the asymmetry without a sustained induction mechanism.

  3. ATP vegetative basin floor:
     CH conditions: vegetative cells park at 2.22 mM (natural) and 2.22 mM
     (abrupt) — consistent with the 2.13 mM topology-derived prediction.
     SM conditions: all sporulate — no vegetative cohort to measure floor.

  4. σ_H_max:
     SM: 1.635–1.647 µM (above θ_σH=1.60 ✅ → crosses gate)
     CH: 1.464–1.553 µM (below threshold → bet-hedging via stochastic septation)
     Confirms: in CH medium σ_H never reliably crosses the commitment gate.

  FUJITA-2 SUITABILITY CHECK (run before FUJITA-2):
  Model: workspace/projects/PLOS-One/models/bacillus_sporulation_v9.shy
  Fixed: LOADING_DOSE=27, INTERVENTION_TIME_S=1 (already at model defaults)

  Phosphatase saturation curve:
    T_Spo0A_dephosphorylation rate = 0.01 × Spo0A_P × (1 + 9×N/(100+N))
    N0=100  → phosphatase factor = 5.5  (55% of max)
    N0=400  → phosphatase factor = 8.6  (86% of max)
    N0=1440 → phosphatase factor = 9.4  (94% of max)  ← near saturation
    N0=2200 → phosphatase factor = 9.6  (96% of max)  ← essentially same
  → Phosphatase is SATURATED above N0~400 µM. The N0 axis in FUJITA-2
    primarily controls how fast nutrients DEPLETE (→ ATP floor crossing),
    not how fast the pulse is cleared. FUJITA-2 is therefore a valid test
    of the ATP basin floor mechanism.

  Expected FUJITA-2 shape (LOADING_DOSE=27, N0 sweep):
    N0=100   → ~100% (nutrients deplete fast, ATP drops below floor)
    N0=300   → ~100% (still fast depletion)
    N0=600   → ~80-90% (moderate depletion)

    N0=2200  → ~5-10% (nutrients barely deplete in 6h)
  The crossover ~50% is the effective ATP basin floor boundary.
