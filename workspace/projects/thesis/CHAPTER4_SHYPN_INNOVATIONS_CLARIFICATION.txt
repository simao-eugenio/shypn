CONCEPTUAL CLARIFICATION:
=========================

CLASSICAL PETRI NET EXTENSIONS:
--------------------------------
1. Test Arcs (F_t)
   - Read-only access to place markings
   - Non-consumptive participation
   - Enzyme modeling: M'(enzyme) = M(enzyme)
   - EXISTS in literature since 1970s-1980s
   - Foundation for regulatory mechanisms

2. Inhibitor Arcs
   - Threshold-based blocking
   - Enabling condition: M(p) < threshold
   - Allosteric regulation modeling
   - STANDARD extension in Petri net theory
   - Not novel to SHYpn

SHYPN INNOVATIONS:
------------------
1. Signal Places (Ψ) + Signal Arcs (F_s)
   - NOVEL: Dual participation mechanism
   - Consumptive + Regulatory sensing simultaneously
   - M'(ATP) = M(ATP) - W_s (token removal)
   - AND concentration sensing for rate modulation
   - GOES BEYOND test arcs (non-consumptive) and inhibitor arcs (blocking)
   - Key innovation: Metabolites as both currency AND signal

2. Hierarchy Layers (λ: P ∪ T → ℕ)
   - NOVEL: Multi-scale biological organization
   - Explicit layer assignments: λ(ATP) = 0, λ(Glucose) = 1, λ(F16BP) = 2
   - Cross-layer regulatory arcs: Layer 0 → Layers 1-2
   - Control architecture made topology-visible
   - Captures biological reality: energy currency regulates pathways

3. Cross-Layer Regulation
   - NOVEL: Regulatory arcs spanning hierarchy levels
   - Signal_flow arcs connecting Layer 0 (ATP/ADP) to Layers 1-2 (reactions)
   - Consumptive semantics + hierarchy constraints
   - Biological: Universal currencies regulate pathway-specific reactions
