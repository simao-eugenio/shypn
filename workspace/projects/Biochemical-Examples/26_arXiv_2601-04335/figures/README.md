# Figures - arXiv 2601.04335

Published figures for **"Thermodynamic Constraints Drive Hierarchical Preemption in Cellular Decision-Making: A Hybrid Petri Net Framework with Application to Bacillus subtilis Sporulation"**

## Figure List

### Figure 1: Thermodynamic Landscape
**File:** `thermodynamic_landscape.pdf`

**Description:** Phase portrait showing energy-minimizing decision pathways in B. subtilis sporulation. Demonstrates how thermodynamic constraints create basin of attraction geometry that determines commitment thresholds.

**Key Features:**
- ATP-dependent phase space (horizontal: ATP, vertical: Spo0A-P)
- Basin of attraction boundaries
- Energy landscape contours
- Commitment threshold line at 2.38 mM ATP

**Generated by:** `../scripts/generate_thermodynamic_landscape.py`

---

### Figure 2: Basin of Attraction
**File:** `bacillus_basin_of_attraction.pdf`

**Description:** Quantitative analysis of basin geometry showing how ATP concentration determines attraction to sporulation vs. vegetative growth states.

**Key Features:**
- Basin width vs. ATP concentration
- Critical threshold identification (2.38 mM)
- Bistability region (1-3 mM)
- Monostable regions (< 1 mM: vegetative, > 3 mM: sporulation)

**Validation:**
- SHYPN prediction: 2.38 mM
- Experimental (Fujita & Losick 2005): 2.21 ± 0.18 mM
- **Error: 7%**

**Generated by:** `../scripts/plot_basin_attraction.py`

---

### Figure 3: Decision Cascade
**File:** `decision_cascade.pdf`

**Description:** Hierarchical layer architecture showing signal flow from environmental sensors through regulatory integration to execution machinery.

**Layers:**
- **Layer 0:** Environmental (ATP/GTP sensing)
- **Layer 1:** Integration (Spo0A phosphorelay)
- **Layer 2:** Commitment (Spo0A-P threshold)
- **Layer 3:** Execution (sigma factor deployment)

**Key Concept:** Signal consumption at Layer 2 creates irreversible commitment point, enabling hierarchical preemption of lower metabolic layers.

**Generated by:** `../scripts/plot_decision_cascade.py`

---

### Figure 4: Stress Response
**File:** `bacillus_sporulation_stress.pdf`

**Description:** Time-series comparison of sporulation under normal vs. stress conditions, demonstrating 16× ATP efficiency improvement through hierarchical preemption.

**Key Results:**

| Condition | ATP Level | Efficiency | Spore Yield |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Normal** | 5000 mM | 11.6 mM/spore | 75 mM (100%) |
| **Stress** | 300 mM (94% depletion) | 0.73 mM/spore | 67 mM (89%) |
| **Improvement** | -94% | **16× better** | -11% |

**Crisis Management Features:**
- ATP minimum: 1 mM (99.7% depletion) - system operates at extreme limit
- Recovery via continuous ATP regeneration during sporulation
- GTP accumulation: +4974 mM (166% increase) - alternative energy buffer
- Demonstrates robust performance under severe energy stress

**Generated by:** Model simulation using `bacillus_sporulation_stress.shy`
