❌ BUG 1: Color Doesn't Change
   Symptom: Arc stays black after transforming to test arc
   Impact: User can't visually distinguish arc types
   
❌ BUG 2: Behavior Mixing
   Symptom: Test arc consumes tokens OR normal arc doesn't consume
   Impact: Simulation produces wrong results
   
❌ BUG 3: Stale Behavior Cache
   Symptom: After transformation, behavior matches OLD type not NEW type
   Impact: Mixed behaviors during successive transformations
   
❌ BUG 4: Weight Loss
   Symptom: Arc weight resets to 1 after transformation
   Impact: High-weight arcs become low-weight
   
❌ BUG 5: Threshold Loss
   Symptom: Inhibitor threshold expression cleared after transformation
   Impact: Inhibitor behavior becomes incorrect
